## SERVICES — FULL BREAKDOWN

### 1. PRINTING SERVICES

**A. Managed Print Services (MPS)**
- Thorough needs assessment of current document output environment
- Analyzes printing infrastructure, user requirements, and workflow
- Selective or general hardware replacement as needed
- Service, genuine parts, and high-quality supplies provided
- Supports existing third-party equipment
- Continuous monitoring of printer, fax, copier, and MFP fleet
- Usage pattern tracking and problem resolution
- Free evaluation offered to companies with existing printer fleets
- Fixed monthly charges available (outsourcing model)
- "Best rates in the industry" + exceptional after-sales support

**B. Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)**
- Covers office printer maintenance
- Free labor costs — all technician labor included at no charge
- Two plan options:
  - AMC WITHOUT Parts: labor covered, spare parts billed separately at preferential rates
  - AMC WITH Parts: everything covered except physical damage to machine
- Focus on predictable, worry-free maintenance costs

**C. Printers (Sales)**
- Affordable inkjet photo printers
- High-quality full-color laser printers
- Multifunctional all-in-one printers (print, scan, copy)
- Any brand and size available
- New and refurbished (with warranty) options
- Home, home office, and corporate options

**D. KG Premium Toner (Own Brand)**
- KG's own brand of remanufactured/premium toner cartridges
- USPs: Quality, durability, long-lasting performance
- Smart, cost-effective, eco-friendly
- Available for a wide range of printer models
- Heritage product — the original business the company was built on

**E. Support and Troubleshooting**
- Free expert advice over the phone
- Diagnosis from: sample prints, screen errors, video observations
- Available to both existing clients and potential clients
- Experienced technicians
